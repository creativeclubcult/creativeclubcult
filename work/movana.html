MOVANA

Campaign · Photography · Film 2026

For this campaign, we wanted to explore a visual language rooted in the world of classic sports advertising: bold compositions, direct lighting, strong silhouettes, and a sense of movement that feels immediate.

Rather than making the campaign feel overly polished, we leaned into a more nostalgic approach. Mixing studio portraits with outdoor environments, we played with hard light, natural textures, and candid moments to create something that feels familiar, but still belongs to the present.

The result is a series of images and moving frames that put the body, the garment, and movement at the centre of the story. From the first frame to the last, every element was built to feel like part of the same visual world.

A campaign shaped around movement, with a little nod to the sports imagery that came before us.
