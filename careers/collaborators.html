How it works

Send your work once

Portfolio links, your crafts, city and rates. Companies and studios apply too, with the gear and team they bring.

We keep you on file

Your details join the CCC network, sorted by craft and city, so the right people get the right call.

We call with a brief

When a production fits, we reach out with the brief and the dates. You say yes, no, or let's talk.

What to send

Your work
Portfolio or showreel links. At least one is needed.
Your crafts
Pick everything you do, so we know when to call.
Rates
Per day or per project, if you'd like to share them.
Gear and team
Equipment, studio space or crew you bring with you.
