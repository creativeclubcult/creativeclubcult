The team.

Four kinds of work, one team. Find where you fit.

CCC works the way a project moves: think, direct, create, produce. These are the parts of the studio we hire for, and open roles show up under each one as they come.

Think

Strategy and words

Open applications welcome

The people who find the angle before anything gets made. Research, positioning and the idea that holds a project together, written so a client can see it and a team can run with it.

Direct

Creative direction

Open applications welcome

The people who set the point of view: concept, tone and story. They lead shoots and edits, keep every decision on brief and protect the idea from first sketch to final cut.

Create

Design and craft

Open applications welcome

The people who make the work. Brand systems, design, motion, photography and content, made with taste and an obsessive eye for detail.

Produce

Production

Open applications welcome

The people who turn plans into shoots, and shoots into work that ships. Budgets, schedules, crews, locations and delivery, run calmly at the standard the idea deserves.

For every role

Type
Full-time and contract, set per role.
Where
On site, remote or hybrid, set per role. Productions take us wherever the work is.
What to send
Portfolio or showreel links, and a CV if you have one. Files can be PDF, JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 20 MB each.
What happens next
We read every application. If there's a fit, we'll reach out to set up a conversation. If there isn't one yet, you stay in our network for the next role.
